1. The service

Archangel Business AI is a business-development workspace for people who are starting, planning or already running a small business. It helps you capture a Start Pack, plan the work, and keep records in one place. It is not a bank, a law firm, an accounting practice, or a substitute for qualified professional advice.

2. Who may use it

The app is a public product. Anyone who is old enough to start or run a business may create an account and set up one or more of their own businesses. It is not directed at children.

3. Your account and your work

You are responsible for the information you enter and for keeping your sign-in details safe. In version 0.2.0, your account and workspace are stored on your device. You can export a copy of your data, or delete your account and workspaces, from Settings. Deletion on the device cannot be undone.

4. Drafts, AI and confirmation

When AI features are connected, outputs will be labelled as drafts unless you accept them. Nothing should be published or sent without your confirmation. In this tester build, Ultra Intelligence does not yet call a live model, and Launch Room does not message or publish on your behalf.

The app will not invent interviews, testimonials, demand, sales or live business metrics. You stay in charge of what is treated as evidence.

5. Money, payroll and official lodgements

Planning scenarios and posted books are kept separate. Amounts in accounts and invoices are only those you enter. The app may help you prepare Australian records such as GST, BAS, super and Single Touch Payroll, but a person still needs to check them — often a registered tax or BAS agent — before anything is lodged. Real STP lodgement needs an ATO-listed STP-enabled provider. AI bots on the Team Family Tree are never paid through payroll.

6. Plans, credits and payments

Free, Pro and Team plans are described in the app and on this site. Prices are not listed yet and will be published with the public launch. Credits, when they are issued, are for service use only and cannot be cashed out. This version does not process payments or collect card details. Paid plans, if offered later, are expected to go through Google Play Billing and will be described before they go live.

7. Acceptable use

Use the app for your own lawful business planning and records. Do not use it to mislead others, to invent customer evidence, or to attempt to access another person’s workspace.

8. No warranty of a business outcome

The app can help you plan and organise the work. It does not promise that a venture will succeed, that a price is correct, or that a lodgement is complete. Use of the app is at your own risk, so far as the law allows.

9. Changes

These draft terms may change as the product matures, especially when cloud sign-in or paid plans are switched on. An updated version will be provided with the app and this website.
