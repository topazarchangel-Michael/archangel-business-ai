Privacy policy (draft)

This is a draft for review. It is not yet a published legal policy and should be checked before a Play Store listing goes live. Questions: TopazArchangel@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Archangel Business AI is a business-development app for founders and small teams. It is developed by Archangel Vault AI. Questions about this draft, or about personal information, can be sent to TopazArchangel@gmail.com.

2. Who the app is for

The app is built as a public product. Anyone can create an account, set up one or more of their own businesses, and keep their work in a private workspace. It is not a personal tool limited to a single business.

3. What this version stores

In version 0.2.0, your information is stored on your device. That includes:

  • The name and email address you use to create an account
  • A one-way hash of your password, with a salt (the password itself is not stored)
  • Business records you choose to enter, such as a Start Pack, workbooks, opportunities, canvas notes, tasks, calendar events, team and family-tree details, invoices, account entries, payroll drafts, and launch checklists

The app does not require you to enter more than you need in order to use it.

4. What this version does not do

This build:

  • Does not upload your business workspace to a server
  • Does not send chat notes to an AI model (Ultra Intelligence is a stub in this version)
  • Does not process payments or collect card details
  • Does not sell your information
  • Does not use your workspace to advertise to you

A later release may offer optional cloud sign-in (for example through Supabase) and paid plans through Google Play Billing. Those features will be described in an updated policy, and will remain off until you choose them.

The Android package may include an internet permission because of the way the app is packaged. Version 0.2.0 does not use that permission to send your business data away from the device.

5. Your controls

From Settings you can:

  • Sign out
  • Export a JSON copy of your profile and workspaces (password hashes are not included)
  • Delete your account and all workspaces saved on the device

Deleting your data on the device cannot be undone. If cloud accounts are added later, deletion of server-side data will be explained at that time.

6. Children

The app is intended for people who are starting or running a business, and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If how the app handles information changes, this draft will be updated and the in-app policy screen will be updated with it.
